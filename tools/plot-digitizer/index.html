Tips for accurate results

  • Use the sharpest image you can — zoom the PDF to 200–400% before taking the screenshot.
  • Place markers on labelled ticks far apart (first and last tick). The small previews show the number at each marker.
  • Legend or text in the way? Use Erase area and drag a box over it.
  • Several curves in the same colour (e.g. all black)? Use Click a curve and tap the one you want.
  • Log-scale axis? Tick “Log x-axis” / “Log y-axis” and type the real values (e.g. 0.01 and 100).
  • A LAMMPS stress_strain.txt made by our MD Paper → LAMMPS tool can be dropped straight into step 2.
