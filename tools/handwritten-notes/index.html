Tips for the best results

        
    
          
  • One page per photo, taken straight from above. Crop away the desk with ✂ Crop on the page card.
    • 
          
  • Faint pencil? Switch that page to Black & white — it boosts the contrast the most.
    • 
          
  • Check the highlighted words with Next to check; turn on Photos beside text to compare with the original page.
    • 
          
  • Diagrams are cut out of your photos automatically. Click a figure in the preview to adjust its crop, or add one with ✂ Diagram.
    • 
          
  • Your work is saved in this browser as you type, so a refresh won't lose the text.
    • 
          
  • Already have a typed PDF or Word file? Use PDF / Word → Markdown instead.
